Privacy Policy

Schuit Sharing · Last updated July 28, 2026

Schuit Sharing (sharing.schuit.io, the “Service”) is a private, invite-only file-sharing application operated by Sturgeon Technologies (“we”, “us”). Access is granted by invitation only. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how you can have it removed.

Information we collect

  • Account identity. When you sign in, we store your email address and, if your sign-in provider supplies it, your display name. This is the entirety of the profile data we keep about you.
  • How you signed in. You may authenticate with Google, with Facebook, or with an email address and password. Sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito. When you use Facebook Login, we request only the public_profile and email permissions, and we receive only your name and email address from Facebook — nothing else, and we never post to your account.
  • Files you access or upload. Files shared through the Service are stored in a private Amazon S3 bucket. We do not inspect file contents; access is limited to the folders an administrator has granted to your account.
  • Access records. We keep basic records needed to run the Service, such as your group membership and which shared folders you are permitted to use.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, we do not run third-party ad or analytics trackers, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and keep the Service invite-only.
  • To determine which shared folders and files you may access.
  • To send account-related email, such as your invitation and one-time verification codes.
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.

How your information is shared

We share personal data only with the infrastructure providers that run the Service on our behalf: Amazon Web Services (Cognito, S3, DynamoDB, Lambda, CloudFront; region us-east-1) and your chosen identity provider (Google or Facebook) at the moment you sign in. We disclose information otherwise only if required by law.

Data retention

We retain your account identity and access records for as long as your account remains active. Pending invitations expire automatically. When your account is deleted, the associated identity data is removed as described below.

Deleting your data

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. This also serves as our data-deletion instructions for Facebook Login users.

  • Email riley.schuit@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, or with the name of the account to remove.
  • We will delete your Cognito account and the identity data we hold (email address and display name) within 30 days and confirm by reply.
  • You can also remove the app's access to your Facebook data at any time from Facebook → Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email riley.schuit@gmail.com.
